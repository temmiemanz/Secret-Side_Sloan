Hollow — Embodiment EGG

Sloan leaves her body and enters another. What can be possessed is anything Sloan believes has a soul. What has a soul is never defined — she has to find out herself.

While Possessing

She sees, hears, and controls the host completely. Her own body stays exactly where she left it — limp, breathing, and completely unprotected. She has no control over it until she returns.

Entry
Host is unconscious
No roll needed
Host is awake but unaware of Sloan
MND roll — need 12 or more
Host is aware of Sloan in any way
MND roll — need 17 or more
Leaving

She can leave at any time but it takes about a minute. She cannot exit instantly to dodge damage. If the host dies or goes unconscious while she is inside, she is ejected immediately and takes 1d8 damage. One host at a time — leaving ends control immediately.

Embodiment Hosts

If the host has an Embodiment type ability, Sloan can use it while inside. She will know nothing about what it does or how it works. It cannot harm her soul.

Lore ▼

Sloan's parents have been addicted since she was little — drugs, alcohol, all of it. Not cruel, just completely absent. The kind of neglect where she could come home at 4am after crawling through abandoned buildings all night and nobody would even look up. Nobody asked. Nobody noticed. Nobody cared.

She got bullied badly enough in elementary school to remember it years later. Middle school looked like it might be different — she found a friend group, let herself get comfortable, told them things about her home life and what it was actually like. They left. Then they told everyone. That was the last time Sloan let anyone that close. She decided it wouldn't happen again and she's held to it.

Grades dropped after that. She's smart — she knows she's smart — but she stopped being able to see the point. Why work toward a future she wasn't sure she'd have? So instead she just moved through days, one at a time, no plan, no people. The thing that actually lights her up is urban exploring. Abandoned buildings at 2am, paranormal investigations alone with a camera, stray cats that let her pet them. She feeds every stray she finds. She always has something in her pockets for them.

She doesn't drink, doesn't smoke, won't touch anything her parents touch. That's a hard line and it doesn't move. She's incredibly awkward when people actually speak to her — which almost never happens, because she won't start it. She compares her problems to everyone else's and usually decides hers aren't serious enough to take up space. She's working on that. Slowly. Mostly by not working on it at all and just going out into the dark with a flashlight looking for something that goes bump.
